What Happens In Each Phase

Months 1–3 · Prove the floor

  • Close on the first tranche; open the operating account with dual control; NDAs signed by CPA and HR.
  • Hire the three offshore closers (or one US closer — option B); dialer, scripts, QC and the pay engine already run.
  • Buy transfers to fill the roster; measure close rate, first-pay clear and cost per deal by vendor.
  • Bank dips to about $67,957 and recovers from month 5.

Months 4–9 · Reserve, then first hire

  • Reserve target (six months of overhead plus chargeback buffer) is met in month 6.
  • Hire signal fires in month 6: occupancy at 100% two months running and the bank covers three months of the next seat.
  • First direct-mail test drop (Tier 1, $15k) only once the reserve is met and only against the kill criteria on page 4.

Months 10–24 · Compound

  • Each new closer repeats the same test: fill them with transfers first, mail second.
  • Your payback completes from distributions once the reserve is held; the waterfall on page 5 governs the order.
  • Bank at month 12: $273,316; at month 24: $639,860 — before any distribution, founder pay or Tier 2 marketing is taken out.
Month-24 bank is the model's gross figure; the waterfall (reserve → founder pay → your payback → 70/30) is applied to it in practice.

Items I Want In The Agreement That We Have Not Discussed Yet
  • Tax distributions — as a partnership you will owe tax on your 30% of allocated profit whether or not cash is distributed; a mandatory quarterly tax distribution at an assumed rate protects you and sits ahead of payback
