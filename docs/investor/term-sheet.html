Term Sheet

Non-binding summary of the principal terms of a proposed investment in Funding Tier LLC, a California limited liability company (the "Company"), by Paul Dillenback or an entity he controls (the "Investor"). Dated November 20, 2026. Except for Confidentiality and Expenses, nothing here is binding; the parties intend to enter into definitive agreements reflecting these terms.

IssuerFunding Tier LLC. The Company's software, program-partner agreements, domains, telephone numbers and accounts remain in the Company; no new entity is formed.
InvestorPaul Dillenback, directly or through an investment entity wholly owned by him.
SecuritiesMembership interests representing, in aggregate, thirty percent (30%) of all membership interests of the Company on a fully issued basis, with the founder, Nathan Pottish, holding seventy percent (70%).
Purchase priceNinety thousand dollars ($90,000), payable in three equal tranches of $30,000: at closing, on the first day of the second month after closing, and on the first day of the third month after closing. Ten percent (10%) of the Company's membership interests are issued upon receipt of each tranche.
Use of proceedsOperating reserve, launch-roster runway through the revenue lag, a direct-mail test program, legal and accounting costs of the transaction and insurance, as described in the Investment Proposal.
DistributionsAvailable cash is applied in the following order: (1) operating costs including rep commissions, bonuses and SPIFFs net of clawbacks and marketing within budget; (2) an operating reserve equal to six months of operating overhead plus a chargeback buffer sized on trailing-twelve-month commission paid; (3) quarterly tax distributions to each member equal to the member's allocated taxable income multiplied by an assumed combined rate set by the Company's accountant; (4) the Manager's performance draw (below); (5) one hundred percent (100%) of remaining distributions to the Investor until the Investor has received $90,000; (6) any accrued and unpaid Manager draw; (7) thereafter seventy percent (70%) to the founder and thirty percent (30%) to the Investor, with the Manager's performance bonus (below) paid quarterly from distributable profit above $20,000 per month.
Manager compensationA monthly draw of $10,000, payable only in a month in which net profit after the draw is positive; increased to $12,500 per month after three consecutive profitable months; a performance bonus of ten percent (10%) of distributable profit in excess of $20,000 per month, paid quarterly, commencing after the Investor has received $90,000; unpaid draw accrues without interest and is paid under step (6) above.
Repurchase optionsThe founder has the option, but not the obligation, to repurchase from the Investor (a) ten percent (10%) of the Company's membership interests during a twelve-month window beginning on the fifth anniversary of closing and (b) a further ten percent (10%) during a twelve-month window beginning on the tenth anniversary, in each case for cash at closing of the repurchase. The window in (a) opens on the third anniversary if the Investor has by then received $180,000 in cumulative distributions. Repurchase price for each ten percent slice equals ten percent of Company value less a twenty percent (20%) discount, where Company value equals three (3.0) times trailing-twelve-month net profit after Manager compensation and before distributions, per the Company's books; no window opens until twelve profitable months exist. The Investor retains ten percent (10%) unless he elects to sell.

Management, approvals, rights and back-office arrangements.

ManagementThe Company is manager-managed. Nathan Pottish is the Manager with authority over sales operations, program partners, product and software, compliance, rep hiring and compensation within the approved budget, marketing within the approved budget (with a twenty-five percent (25%) flex above the marketing line without a second approval), and vendor selection. The Investor is a member and is not required to perform operating duties.
Actions requiring both membersIncurring debt over $25,000 or any personal guarantee; issuing equity or admitting investors; sale of the Company or of material intellectual property; changing a program partner or the structure of the rep commission plan; hiring above rep level; related-party payments; expansion events (Tier 3 marketing at $90,000 per month or more, an office lease, or any back-end servicing build); marketing spend beyond the flex ceiling; opening any bank, merchant, card or lending account; amending the operating agreement.
Banking and spending controlsThe two members are the only authorized signers on every Company account. Bank-enforced dual approval applies to transactions above $10,000. No cards, ACH credentials or wire authority are issued to any other person. The payroll provider executes only batches approved by a member and holds no standing debit authority. The accountant has view-only access. All accounts are listed on an Accounts Schedule to the operating agreement.
Information rightsContinuous access for the Investor to the Company's operating model, dashboards, reconciliation and commission software, and bank accounts (view); monthly profit and loss by the fifteenth of the following month; quarterly review meeting; annual Schedule K-1 and tax return.
Transfer restrictionsNo transfer of membership interests without a right of first refusal in favor of the other member. Tag-along rights for the Investor on any sale by the founder. Pre-emptive (pro rata) rights for both members on any new issuance; the Investor has the first right to fund future capital needs on the same terms as offered to third parties.
Back-office servicesThe Investor will cause his accounting and human-resources providers to serve the Company at the Investor's expense. The Company's reconciliation and commission software is the computation of record for revenue recognition, commission payable, bonuses, SPIFFs, clawbacks and distribution allocation; the accountant posts from its monthly run and does not independently determine eligibility. The HR provider will respect the Company's independent-contractor, commission-only enrollment-rep model and its state-by-state hiring rules. Providers sign the Company's confidentiality agreement before access.
Expansion events and future capitalExpansion events require both members, a separate budget, and new capital contributed pro rata or raised from third parties subject to the pre-emptive and ROFR rights above. A move into back-end servicing is treated as a separate financing.

Continuity, confidentiality, conditions and acknowledgement.

Death, disability and deadlockOn the death or permanent disability of a member, the other member (or the Company) may purchase the affected interest at the repurchase formula above. The Company will maintain key-person insurance on the Manager. A deadlock on a both-member action is resolved by a mediation step followed by the status quo (no action) unless the operating agreement specifies otherwise.
Non-solicitationDuring membership and for twelve months after, neither member will solicit the Company's enrollment reps, vendors or program partners for a competing enterprise.
Confidentiality and intellectual property (binding)Each party keeps the other's confidential information, including this Term Sheet and the Investment Proposal, confidential, and uses it only to evaluate and complete the transaction. All software, scripts, standard operating procedures, the pay engine, data, lists and creative used in the business are and remain the Company's property; neither member may use or disclose them outside the Company; these obligations survive withdrawal, repurchase or dissolution. The Company holds a written licence to its platform and hosting arrangements.
Tax mattersAdmission of the Investor converts the Company to partnership tax classification (Form 1065 and Schedules K-1). The Company's accountant will confirm the employer identification number, update state filings to list both members, and set the assumed rate for tax distributions.
Conditions to closingExecution of a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement and an Amended and Restated Operating Agreement reflecting these terms, with the Compensation Schedule, Accounts Schedule and Confidentiality / IP exhibits attached; completion of the accountant's tax review; opening of the operating account with dual control; execution of the platform and hosting licence; confidentiality agreements signed by the accounting and HR providers.
Expenses (binding)Each party bears its own expenses. The Company's legal costs for the definitive documents are a Company expense and may be paid from the first tranche.
Governing lawCalifornia.
Non-binding effect; expirationExcept for Confidentiality and Intellectual Property and Expenses, this Term Sheet is a non-binding expression of intent and creates no obligation to complete the transaction. It expires if definitive agreements are not executed within sixty (60) days of the date above, unless extended in writing.
