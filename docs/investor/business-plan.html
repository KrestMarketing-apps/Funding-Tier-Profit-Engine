Executive Summary

Funding Tier LLC is a front-end enrollment company in consumer debt relief. It generates and qualifies calls, matches each caller to the best-fit program, enrolls them, and is paid by the servicing partner as the client pays. It never holds client money and never charges the consumer.

The Model In One Paragraph

A caller reaches a closer — by purchased live transfer today, by direct mail and paid social once those are proven. The closer qualifies for ability to pay, pulls soft credit, and the deal router picks the program the caller fits: Debt Settlement (Level Debt), Debt Validation (Shield Services), Debt Resolution (Elite Legal Practice), with Tax Resolution (Milestone) available on the same call. The partner services the client and pays Funding Tier a contractual share of each client payment — once for settlement, monthly for validation and resolution — unless Funding Tier elects a one-time buyout on a file. Because payouts follow client payments, revenue arrives behind enrollment and compounds as monthly cohorts stack into a book.

Mission

Put every qualified caller into the program that actually fits them, enroll only clients who can pay, and be paid for the outcome rather than the sale. The business wins when clients stay — which is why qualification and call quality are funded before growth marketing.

Position In The Value Chain

Funding Tier is the front end: marketing, intake, qualification, program matching, sales and enrollment. Servicing partners run the programs, hold client funds, negotiate or validate, and remit Funding Tier's share. This keeps Funding Tier out of fund-handling and the licensing that comes with it, and lets it sell four programs from one conversation. A move into back-end servicing is possible later and is defined as an expansion event requiring both members and separate capital.

Market Context

Revolving consumer credit in the United States runs in the trillions of dollars, and a persistent share of households carry balances they cannot retire on their own. Debt relief programs — settlement, validation, attorney-led resolution — are an established category with national servicing partners, which is why a focused front end can exist without building servicing itself. The category is also competitive and regulated, so the durable advantages are qualification quality, retention, cost per deal and compliance discipline rather than volume alone.

Marketing Plan

Three ways to put a qualified caller in front of a closer. Transfers are the cheapest and are bought to fill seats; direct mail and paid social are owned channels that earn a standing budget only after a measured test beats what a deal pays.

Purchased transfers

  • Primary source; ~$75 per paid deal at today's close rate
  • Buffer mix ($8 / $14 / $55 by length) tuned monthly to close rate by buffer
  • Every billed transfer credit-pulled and logged; disputes weekly; two vendors minimum
  • Vendors can supply well beyond the launch roster — supply is not the constraint

Direct mail + prescreen data

  • Bureau-reseller lists (Altair CreditReady, CRS LeadsIQ, Deluxe): high revolving utilization, $20k+ unsecured, no bankruptcy
  • Firm-offer-of-credit structure through the lender partner; declines route to debt programs; attorney review of creative
  • $0.80 all-in per piece; tracking number per drop; two creatives per test
  • Planning: 1.25% response · 70% qualified · 20% close · $25k debt

Paid social

  • Retargets mailed ZIPs only; brand familiarity drives the lower cost per lead
  • UTM on every ad → landing page → contact in app.krestmarketing.com
  • Planning: $35 CPL · 30% qualified · 15% close; stand-alone it does not pay

The Tier 1 Test, And How ROI Is Measured

Three monthly drops of 10,000 pieces, two list segments, two creatives. Every inbound call carries the drop's tracking number, so the contact, the opportunity and the paid deal all inherit the campaign code. Pass: cost per paid deal below what a deal pays after the rep (≈ $524 on US-closed, $683 on offshore-closed) and average debt above $20,000. Kill: two drops above it. After month 3 the measured response, qualified share and close rate replace the planning assumptions in the operating model, and the winning segment goes to Tier 2 only if the pass held.
