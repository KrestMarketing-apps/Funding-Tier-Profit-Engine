Roles — Operator, Investor, Lead, Closers

Everyone knows what is theirs. The operator runs the floor and the system; the investor is a full partner with a seat in operations whenever both agree; the lead arrives at the 4th closer.

Operator — Nathan

Manager; closes daily
  • Closes files alongside the team
  • Trains and monitors up to 3 closers; hires within the roster plan
  • Transfer vendors, partner relationships, compliance
  • Owns the system; publishes the daily briefing, weekly recap, monthly review
  • Marketing within budget (+25% flex)

Investor — Paul

Member; may operate by agreement
  • Full information rights: dashboards, bank view, model, P&L by the 15th
  • Co-signer; one of two approvals above $10,000; one of two votes on both-member actions
  • Brings accounting (now) and HR (when US W-2 hires start)
  • May take an operating role — vendors, hiring, finance, partners — scoped in writing

Team lead

Added at the 4th closer
  • US W-2, $22–$26 / hr + 0.1–0.25% override on team volume
  • Daily huddle, QC review within 24 hours, vendor disputes weekly
  • One lead per 5–6 closers
  • Frees the operator for enrollments and partners

Closers

Egypt BPO (Plan A) · BPO (B) · US W-2 (C)
  • 8-hour shifts inside the operating window
  • Qualify fast: income verification on every file
  • One program for 90 days (Shield), then all three
  • Offshore: $12 / hr + 3–6% KPI bonus · US: draw + commission (Exhibit C)
Hours Of Operation
  • Monday–Friday 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM Pacific
  • Saturday 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM Pacific
  • Closers clock in only inside the window (system-enforced)
  • Transfers are bought to match staffed hours
  • Egypt closers take the US day shift (their evening)
  • After-hours inbound → AI receptionist → next-shift callback

The 90-Day Launch

Shield Services only for 90 days: one program, one script, one partner, so three new closers learn it cold. Transfers at $14 per call, 2-minute buffer, fill the seats from day one.

Also illustrated inBusiness Plan §13 · Growth Plan → Month by Month

Week 0 — Close and set up

  • Accounts opened: operating + high-yield reserve, both members as signers, dual control above $10,000
  • Investor access live: dashboards, model, bank view
  • CallBlade trial agreement: 3 closers, $12 / hr, 1–2 week paid trial
  • Transfer vendor order placed for staffed hours; 2-minute buffer, $14
  • Shield script, SOP and certification assigned to each closer

Weeks 1–2 — Trial

  • Day 1–2: certification on the Shield script, router and income verification
  • Day 3+: live calls; operator listens to every closer's first 10 calls
  • QC on 100% of enrollment calls; coaching daily
  • Pass: ≥ 1 deal per 8 hours by day 10 and QC score ≥ 80
  • Fail: replaced from the same source (CallBlade); Plan B / C only if 3 seats cannot be staffed

Weeks 3–13 — Run and measure

  • Transfers tuned weekly to close rate; disputes filed Friday
  • Buyout election on every validation file (cash first)
  • Month 2: first payouts; month 4: bank back above $90,000
  • Measured first-pay and month-2 data replace placeholders
  • Day 90: open the mix to all three programs; reserve target $138,403 expected by month 5
Training Closers To Qualify Fast
  • Income verification is the payment-propensity test. Employer, pay frequency, next pay date; payment set after the pay date
  • Higher stated debt loads get a two-question check in the first 3 minutes: total unsecured balance and current monthly minimums — if the numbers do not hold together, disqualify kindly and move on
  • Router shows the program and payout once debt, state and account types are entered; no quote before the router
  • Required disclosures read in full; QC scores comprehension, not just completion

The Sales Floor, File Quality And Closer Pay

What a closer does all day, what makes a file count, and how each kind of closer is paid — including the offshore KPI bonus.

Also illustrated inExhibit C · Business Plan §8
A Qualified File
  • Unsecured debt above the program floor; state allowed for the program
  • Income verified; payment date set against the pay cycle
  • No active bankruptcy; no unexplained NSF pattern
  • Agreement signed; first-draft date set; opportunity created with the campaign code
  • QC score ≥ 80 or reviewed before the first draft
Call QC
  • Every enrollment call transcribed and scored (pressure, confusion, inability to pay, disclosures)
  • Flags reviewed by the operator / lead within 24 hours, before the draft
  • Scores feed the weekly recap and the offshore bonus
Offshore / BPO KPI Bonus — How It Is Earned

Hours worked and schedule adherence20%≥ 95% of scheduled hours
QC score (attentiveness, disclosures)25%Average ≥ 85
Deals per 8 hours15%≥ 1.25
  • Goal: 3–6% of monthly hourly pay ($62–$125 on $2,080); plan default 4.5%
  • Paid as a flat % of the revenue the closer generated, scaled by the KPI score; nothing under 70% of targets
  • Computed by the pay engine with the monthly commission run; shown on My Deals & Pay
US W-2 Closer Pay (Plan C)
  • Draw at or above the state minimum wage for every hour (Business Plan §9); HR provider engaged at the first hire
  • Commission per file when it reaches its payout month and survives: settlement 0.75–1.25% of enrolled debt; validation $275–$400; resolution $275–$400
  • Daily-deal bonus $50 / deal at 3+ per day, $75 at 5+; ramp: first 3 months paid at deal-month 3
  • 30-day trial: ≥ 1 deal per 8 hours by day 15, first-pay within 5 points of the floor
Owner-Operator
  • No commission, SPIFF or bonus on his own files; paid by draw and performance bonus under the distribution agreement

Marketing — Test Protocol And The Mix Rule

Transfers are the base. Mail and social are added to replace 25% of transfer volume with larger-debt files once the reserve is met, and never exceed 50% of occupancy.

Also illustrated inGrowth Marketing Budget Plan (project) · Growth Plan → Methods
Before Any Test
  • Reserve target $138,403 met and bank ≥ reserve + 2 months of the test budget
  • Seats have room: occupancy under 95% or a closer added for the inbound
  • List pre-scrubbed (vendor or internal) against DNC and Blacklist Alliance; consent language attorney-reviewed
  • Tracking number and campaign code per drop; source stamped on every contact in app.krestmarketing.com
Tier 1 Tests
ChannelBudget / moTarget cost per fileMinimum run

Scale, Hold Or Cut
  1. Scale after 2 consecutive months at or under the target cost per enrolled file and first-pay within 5 points of transfers — raise the budget 50% at a time
  2. Hold when cost per file is within 25% of target — fix creative, list or script, re-test one month
  3. Cut after 2 months 25% over target, or first-pay 10 points under transfers — stop immediately, keep the learning
The Mix Rule
  • Target: mail + social = 25% of monthly deals once the reserve is met
  • Cap: never above 50% of occupancy or inbound volume — inbound arrives in bursts; transfers are bought by the hour
  • The dashboard shows the share daily; the model warns when a plan breaches 50%
  • Average debt by channel is reported monthly against the sliding scale (Business Plan §12): mail needs ~$20k, social ~$25k
Transfer Vendor Management
  • Standard election $14 / 2-minute buffer; mix changed only on measured close rate by buffer
  • Coached or suspicious transfers tagged on the call and disputed every Friday
  • Second vendor added at stage 3; no vendor above 70% of volume

Money — Monthly Close, Waterfall And Decision Rules

How cash is counted, in what order it moves, and the dollar thresholds that decide every hire, test and distribution.

Also illustrated inInvestment Proposal p.9 and p.12 · Term Sheet
Monthly Waterfall (In Order)
  1. Operating costs — labor, transfers, software, US commission net of clawbacks, offshore bonus, marketing within budget
  2. Operating reserve — 6 months of overhead + chargeback buffer ($138,403 at launch), held in the high-yield account
  3. Tax distributions — each member's share of taxable income × the CPA's assumed rate, quarterly
  4. Manager draw — $10,000 in a profitable month; $12,500 after 3 consecutive profitable months
  5. Investor payback — 100% of what is left to Paul until $90,000 is returned
  6. Accrued draw — any draw skipped in loss months
  7. 70 / 30 — remaining profit; Manager bonus 10% of profit above $20,000 / month, quarterly, after payback
Accounts
  • Operating account: about one month of spend
  • High-yield interest-bearing account: reserve and undistributed proceeds; interest belongs to the Company
  • Both members signers on both; dual control above $10,000 — per transaction, including one-time purchases; split payments to one payee within 7 days count as one
  • A/R (payouts owed on files that have paid) is shown beside the bank but never distributed

Compliance Operations — TCPA, DNC, Litigators And Data

The checklist that runs before, during and after every campaign and call. Examples of each report are in the Funding Tier system under Admin → Compliance.

Also illustrated inBusiness Plan §6

Before a list is touched

  • Pre-scrubbed by the vendor, or scrubbed internally in app.krestmarketing.com
  • Blacklist Alliance litigator and serial-plaintiff scrub
  • National and state DNC, and the Company's universal DNC list
  • Consent basis documented per record (coreg source, form, date, IP)
  • Attorney-reviewed creative and scripts; firm-offer structure on prescreen mail

On every call

  • Transfer vendor consent record attached to the lead
  • Required disclosures read; no advance fee, no outcome promise
  • State exclusion enforced by the router before any quote
  • Opt-out honored immediately across every channel
  • Recording and transcript to call-QC

After

  • Weekly dispute of coached or suspicious transfers
  • Complaint log: route to partner, track to close within 5 days
  • Monthly scrub refresh on any list still in use
  • Prospect financial data stays in the CRM and pay engine; no exports; vendor NDAs
  • E&O policy current ($400–$500 / yr); incident log reviewed monthly

If Something Goes Wrong
  1. Stop the campaign or the closer the same day
  2. Pull the records: list, scrub report, consent, recording
  3. Notify the other member and counsel within 24 hours
  4. Fix the control, document it, re-start only after both members agree
