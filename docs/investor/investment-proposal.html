  • Full visibility. AI-powered dashboards tell both members what to continue, cut or improve — daily briefing, weekly recap, monthly KPI review.
  • Scales without re-platforming. Adding closers, a second transfer vendor or a mail program is configuration, not development.

Where The Calls Come From

Purchased live transfers fill the seats from day one. Mail and paid social cost more per call because the caller is higher-intent: a mail response is a household choosing a loan swap or debt relief; a social call is someone who went looking. Both should carry larger debt, close more often and lean toward settlement — target 25% of volume, never more than 50%.

Live transfers — the standard election

  • $14 per call, 2-minute buffer: we pay only if the caller stays on 2 minutes
  • Cost per deal $93$14 ÷ 15% close
  • Typical enrolled debt $15,000; vendors can fill well beyond the launch roster
  • Closers are trained to qualify or disqualify fast for higher stated debt loads; income verification is the payment-propensity test — no file without it

Direct mail on TransUnion data

  • $25,000 of maxed-out revolving debt is the floor; target band $25,000–$250,000
  • Cost per deal $543$0.80 / piece ÷ 1.25% response ÷ 70% qualified ÷ 20% close
  • Firm-offer-of-credit structure through the lender partner; attorney-reviewed creative
  • Starts after the reserve is met; sized to replace 25% of transfer volume

Paid social, and our own data campaigns

  • Social: $35 / lead, 30% qualified, 15% close → $778 per deal — retargeting of mailed ZIPs only
  • Option: run our own dialing, SMS and ringless-voicemail campaigns on purchased co-registration data through app.krestmarketing.com, scrubbed against DNC and litigator lists first
  • Both are tested at Tier 1 budgets and continued only on measured cost per enrolled file

  • Partners remit in arrears; a late remittance shifts a month, not the total.
  • Tax resolution is modelled at zero — upside only.

Risk Factors And How Each Is Mitigated

Every business in this category faces the same risks. What differs is whether the mitigation is a policy someone remembers or a control the system enforces. Ours are the second kind.

TCPA, do-not-call and litigators

The largest single legal exposure in outbound marketing
  • Pre-scrubbed lists only — from the vendor or scrubbed internally before any dial, text or voicemail
  • Blacklist Alliance litigator and serial-plaintiff scrub on every list
  • Universal DNC management integrated in app.krestmarketing.com; opt-outs honored across every channel in real time
  • Transfer vendors hold written consent records; coached or suspicious transfers disputed weekly
  • Prior express written consent captured on every web form and mail response

Client attrition and chargebacks

Files that stop paying stop paying us
  • Income verification on every file — payment propensity, not just willingness
  • AI call-QC before the first draft; pressure or confusion goes to review
  • Shield-first 90 days (≥85%, 80% buyouts) so closers master one program before all four and early cash is locked in
  • Settlement second-payment NSF (15%) and perpetual cancellations are in every number here
  • Buyout election on validation files when cancellation runs above the break-even (page 12 of Exhibit B)

Partner concentration

Four partners remit our revenue
  • Four programs across four partners (Level Debt, Shield Services, Elite Legal Practice, Milestone Tax Solutions); any one can be replaced without re-platforming
  • Remittances reconciled to the deal within 5 days; a late remittance is chased, not absorbed
  • Changing a partner is a both-member action

Key person

Nathan is operator, closer and builder
  • Every SOP, script and process lives in the system, not in his head
  • Team lead added at the 4th closer; investor may take an operating role
  • Key-person insurance; continuity clause at formula price

Regulatory change

State debt-relief laws and the Telemarketing Sales Rule evolve
  • Partners, not Funding Tier, hold the client funds and program licences
  • Router enforces state exclusions before a quote; the state hiring map does the same for reps
  • No advance fees ever; scripted disclosures; E&O policy in place

Capital and cash

The first month is paid before the first payout
  • Lowest bank $66,933 on the safe case; reserve target in 3 months
  • Hold rule: no hiring while the bank is under the reserve target
  • Reserve and proceeds in a high-yield interest-bearing account under dual control
